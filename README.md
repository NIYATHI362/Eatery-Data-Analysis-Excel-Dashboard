# Eatery-Food-Delivery-Data-Analysis-Excel-Dashboard
This project presents an interactive Food Delivery Analytics Dashboard for Eatery, analyzing 8 months of 2025 data. It visualizes sales, orders, ratings, and regional performance to uncover trends in customer behavior, revenue, and business performance using Excel-based analytics and KPIs.

# Business Objectives
The dashboard was designed to answer key business questions such as:
How are sales performing month by month?
Which days and weeks generate higher sales?
What is the average order value?
How many orders are being received?
How do Veg and Non-Veg orders contribute to total sales?
Which states generate the highest revenue?
Which cities are the top contributors to sales?
How does business performance vary across quarters?
What is the overall customer rating and rating volume?

# Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
The dashboard tracks the following major KPIs:
Total Sales (₹) – Overall revenue generated from food orders.
Average Rating – Average customer rating across restaurants/orders.
Average Order Value (₹) – Average revenue generated per order.
Ratings Count – Total number of customer ratings/reviews.
Total Orders – Total number of food orders received.

# Dashboard Visualizations
The dashboard includes multiple visualizations to analyze different aspects of business performance:
# Sales Analysis
Monthly Sales Trend – Tracks fluctuations in sales across months.
Daily Sales Trend – Analyzes variations in orders and revenue across days.
Weekly Trend Analysis – Identifies weekly sales patterns and peak periods.
# Product/Cuisine Analysis
Total Sales by Food Type – Compares revenue contribution from Veg and Non-Veg categories.
# Geographical Analysis
Total Sales by State – Displays state-wise revenue distribution using a map visualization.
Top 5 Cities by Sales – Identifies the cities contributing the highest revenue.
# Performance Analysis
Quarterly Performance Summary – Compares Sales, Ratings, and Orders across different quarters.

The dashboard uses Excel Slicers to allow users to dynamically filter and explore the data.

Users can interact with the dashboard to analyze performance across different categories and time periods, making it easier to identify trends and compare business performance.
